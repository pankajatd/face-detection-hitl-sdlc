    Real-World Face Detection Failure Modes

    1. Environmental Flaws
    • Severe Low Light: Mean luminance < 35; facial contours blend into background shadows.
    • Motion Blur: Rapid subject motion or camera shake causes edge gradients to flatten.
    • Dynamic Range: Blown out highlights or deep shadows baffle rigid detection heuristics.
    2. False Positive Noise
    • Spectacle Rims: Dark glasses frames trigger miniature nested face boxes over eyes/nose.
    • Clothing Patterns: Checkered shirts, ties, and knitwear sweaters frequently detected as faces.
    • Non-Face Clutter: Wall textures and background objects produce persistent false alarms.
    3. Crowd & Merge Errors
    • Couple / Adjacent Faces: Two people standing side-by-side merged into one huge bounding box.
    • Scale Variations: Distant faces completely omitted by fixed-scale scanning cascades.
    • Single-Pass Flaw: Traditional pipelines execute once, fail silently, and exit.
    Architecture Overview

    LangGraph StateGraph Self-Healing Architecture

    Phase 1
    Input & Perception
    • Image Ingestion: Loaded into standardized AgentState schema.
    • YuNet Face Detector: Multi-scale ONNX neural network scans for faces & landmarks.
    • Coordinates Stored: [x, y, w, h] bounding boxes recorded in state.
    Phase 2
    Diagnostic Inspection
    • Laplacian Variance: Quantifies sharpness (blur threshold < 100).
    • Mean Luminance: Quantifies illumination (< 40 dark; > 210 blown).
    • Decision Routing: Flaws detected & Iter < 3 ➔ Branch to Enhancer.
    Phase 3
    Healing & Audit
    • Autonomous Healing: CLAHE brightness boost or Unsharp Mask applied.
    • Re-Detection Loop: Enhanced buffer sent back to FaceDetectorAgent.
    • Test Auditor: Computes IoU against ground truth & issues quality score.
    Agent Roles & Responsibilities

    Meet the 4 Autonomous Agents

    1. FaceDetectorAgent
    src/detection/face_detector.py
    • Executes OpenCV YuNet Deep Neural Network (MobileNet backbone ONNX).
    • Extracts bounding boxes with 5 landmark locations (eyes, nose, mouth corners).
    • Configured with NMS threshold 0.30 and confidence threshold 0.40.
    2. QualityInspectorAgent
    src/quality/inspector.py
    • Computes mathematical blur metric using cv2.Laplacian(gray).var().
    • Computes luminance mean and contrast standard deviation.
    • Filters false-positive spectacle sub-boxes via spatial containment tests.
    3. ImageEnhancerAgent
    src/enhancement/enhancer.py
    • Applies Adaptive CLAHE (L-channel boost in LAB color space).
    • Executes Unsharp Masking kernel to sharpen degraded edges.
    • Records full transformation audit history in AgentState.
    4. TestAuditorAgent
    src/auditing/auditor.py
    • Ground-truth validation: Computes Intersection-over-Union (IoU).
    • Calculates composite quality index (0-100) based on resolution & clarity.
    • Issues final certification: PASSED, WARNING, or FAILED.

    Precision Engineering

    Multi-Face Separation & Spectacle Suppression

    Adjacent Two-Face Separation
    • The Challenge: In couple or group portraits, adjacent heads often merge into one large box.
    • Calibrated NMS Stride: Configured Non-Maximum Suppression with iou_thresh = 0.55.
    • Anchor Separation: Independent landmark centroid detection prevents merging of neighboring proposals.
    • Verified Result: Discrete, tightly-bound boxes [x1, y1, w1, h1] and [x2, y2, w2, h2].
    Nested Spectacle Suppression
    • The Challenge: Dark glasses rims create high-contrast rectangular contours inside the face.
    • Containment Metric: Evaluates intersection area: Area(A ∩ B) / Area(B) > 0.70.
    • Area Ratio Guard: Sub-boxes smaller than 25% of the enclosing face are discarded as artifacts.
    • Verified Result: Zero nested false alarms even with dark sunglasses and thick frames.
